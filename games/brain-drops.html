About Brain Drops

A cosmic jar of objects is hurtling towards the town of Brain Drops. It is your job to protect the town by typing the object names to destroy them.

With over 100 different objects to identify, multiple achievements to unlock and different backgrounds to acquire, this game has a lot waiting for you!

Challenge friends to exciting one-on-one wagers for GO Coins or casually blast your way through waves of earth-bound items in single play mode.

  • 100+ objects to identify
  • Achievements to unlock
  • Single play or one-on-one

Plays in the browser on desktop. Keyboard recommended.
