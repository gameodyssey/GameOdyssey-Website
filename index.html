We are what we play

GameOdyssey (GO for short) unites fun-loving Earthlings through a variety of free and cool social games.

GO is a London-based studio that develops and distributes games across multiple platforms. Our classic catalogue now plays straight from your browser: no download, no sign-up, just hit play.

Our mission is to build exciting games that Odysseans can enjoy alone or among friends.
